📱 App Installation Guide

Install CreteXchange on your phone for quick, one-tap access. No app store needed!

📱 iOS (iPhone/iPad) Safari Browser

1
Open Safari
Visit creteexchange.com (or your app URL) in Safari browser
2
Tap the Share Button
Look for the Share icon at the bottom of your screen
📤 (Square with arrow pointing up)
3
Select "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the menu and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4
Customize & Add
Edit the name if desired (e.g., "CreteXchange")
Tap Add in the top-right corner
5
Done! ✅
The CreteXchange icon now appears on your home screen.
Tap it anytime for instant access!
⚠️ Don't see "Add to Home Screen"?
  • Scroll to the very bottom of the Share menu
  • Tap "Edit Actions"
  • Find "Add to Home Screen" and tap the green + button
  • Go back and try again

📱 Android Chrome Browser

1
Open Chrome
Visit creteexchange.com in Chrome browser
2
Open Menu
Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner
3
Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
• For standard sites: Choose "Add to Home screen"
• For PWAs: Choose "Install app" (recommended)
4
Confirm Installation
Edit the app name if desired
Tap Add or Install
5
Done! ✅
The app icon appears on your home screen AND in your app drawer

📱 Android Firefox Browser

1
Open Firefox
Visit creteexchange.com in Firefox browser
2
Open Menu
Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the toolbar
3
Add to Home Screen
Expand the "More" section
Select "Add to Home Screen"
4
Place Icon
Edit the shortcut name if desired
Tap Add Automatically (or drag to place manually)
💡 Quick Tip - Firefox Alternative Method
Touch and hold the URL in the address bar, then select "Add Page Shortcut"

✨ Benefits of Installing

  • One-tap access - Launch instantly from your home screen
  • App-like experience - Full-screen mode without browser bars
  • Faster loading - Optimized performance and caching
  • Offline support - Access key features without internet
  • Push notifications - Stay updated on washout approvals (if enabled)
🔧 Troubleshooting

iOS: "Add to Home Screen" option missing?

  • Make sure you're using Safari (not Chrome or other browsers)
  • Check if option is disabled in Edit Actions menu
  • Try closing and reopening Safari

Android: Icon doesn't appear after installation?

  • Check your app drawer (swipe up from home screen)
  • Long-press home screen and look in widgets/shortcuts section
  • Restart your device if needed

Icon looks wrong or missing?

  • Try removing and reinstalling the app
  • Clear browser cache and try again
  • Make sure you're on the latest browser version

🗑️ How to Remove

iOS:

  • Touch and hold the app icon
  • Tap "Remove App" → "Delete App"

Android:

  • Long-press the app icon
  • Drag to "Remove" or "Uninstall"
  • For installed PWAs: Also check Settings → Apps
