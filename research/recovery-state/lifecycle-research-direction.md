# Adopted NSF research direction: lifecycle recovery intelligence

Status: Approved strategic research direction, November 2, 2026. This is not a finding of NSF eligibility or technical novelty, and does not authorize production changes or grant submission.

## Positioning
CreteXchange is the driver-first operational foundation of a proposed construction-material recovery and lifecycle intelligence platform. Its differentiation hypothesis is direct driver participation, facility discovery, drop documentation, and incentive-supported evidence collection, rather than dependence solely on general contractors or other centralized actors.

Complementary technology already developed for outbound material ticketing may support a larger solution. The NSF narrative need not name the separate product. Existing outbound capabilities include operator-entered quantities, loaded-truck photographs, electronic signatures, printed receipts, and email records. Truck weighbridge connectivity is planned, not verified as implemented.

## Lifecycle scope
1. Construction material generation and transport
2. Driver-initiated facility discovery and documented delivery
3. Facility acceptance, commingled stockpiling, and processing
4. Recovered-material outbound distribution and measurement
5. Evidence reconciliation, recovery estimates, uncertainty, and environmental intelligence

## Central research question
Can a driver-initiated, multi-party evidence network reliably distinguish observed delivery, independently supported acceptance, aggregate recovery, and unknown individual-load attribution when records are incomplete, measurements heterogeneous, and material identity lost through mixing?

## Research gaps to assess
- Independence and correlation of driver, facility, and transaction evidence
- Inbound receipts: electronic and handwritten
- Processing and stockpile inventory records
- Outbound ticket quantities: operator-entered volume, truck-capacity estimates, and measured weighbridge weight
- Material-specific conversions, timing, inventory changes, and uncertainty
- Attribution limits and defensible environmental claims
- Comparison against existing evidence fusion, mass-balance, material-flow, and statistical methods

## Gates before a second NSF Project Pitch
1. Verify competitor and prior-art differentiation, including driver-first methods.
2. Inventory real facility records with permission; separate observed, estimated, and missing facts.
3. Identify at least one reproducible failure of established technical baselines.
4. Define a testable, genuinely new technical method and high-risk experiment.
5. Obtain independent technical review; only then decide whether to submit.

## Guardrails
This research direction does not merge Draft PR #7, modify production systems, change billing, or assert that a working product by itself demonstrates NSF technical novelty. Operational approval does not prove physical recovery of a specific inbound load. Research claims must distinguish implemented functionality, proposed integrations, and hypotheses.
