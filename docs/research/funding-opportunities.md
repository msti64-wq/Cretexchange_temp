# CreteXchange Funding Opportunities

**Status:** Opportunity-Monitoring Framework
**Important:** This document does not assert that any program is open, eligible, or suitable. Current opportunities must be verified from official sources before action.

## Evaluation Criteria

For every opportunity, record:

- sponsoring organization and official source
- program and solicitation identifier
- current open/close dates and timezone
- applicant and entity eligibility
- topic fit and explicit exclusions
- research versus deployment expectations
- award type, period, cost share, indirect-cost rules, and budget limits
- intellectual-property, data-rights, publication, and reporting terms
- required partners, registrations, letters, and certifications
- environmental-justice, regional, or public-benefit requirements
- submission components and internal review dates
- go/no-go rationale and owner

## Opportunity Classes

### NSF

Monitor programs supporting high-risk technical innovation and commercialization research. Use the [NSF Project Pitch](./nsf-project-pitch.md) and [Phase I Research Plan](./nsf-phase1-research-plan.md) only after verifying current program rules and fit.

### EPA Partnerships and Grants

Monitor official opportunities related to materials management, recycling, data, pollution prevention, environmental justice, regional capacity, or research partnerships when scopes align. Do not imply EPA endorsement or eligibility.

### DOE and DOT

Evaluate only when current topics align with construction materials, transportation efficiency, infrastructure, geospatial analysis, industrial systems, or related research. Avoid forcing platform language into an unrelated solicitation.

### Texas and TCEQ-Related Opportunities

Maintain a dated calendar of official state, regional, and TCEQ-related programs where material recovery, recycling, planning, research, or public benefit align. Confirm geographic and entity eligibility.

### Municipal and Regional Partnerships

Explore bounded pilots, studies, and service agreements with cities, counties, councils of governments, and public works organizations. Define procurement, public-records, confidentiality, aggregation, data ownership, and publication terms.

### Universities and Research Organizations

Seek investigators whose expertise matches the research questions. Establish principal-investigator roles, facilities, ethics review, data agreements, publication, IP, and student involvement before proposal submission.

### Industry Sponsorship and Foundations

Consider sponsored research or mission-aligned foundation funding with explicit independence, conflicts, data rights, publication, and participant-protection terms.

### Investors and Strategic Partners

Use research and commercialization evidence for diligence, but do not present grant hypotheses, future markets, model performance, or environmental outcomes as established facts.
