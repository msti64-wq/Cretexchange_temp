# CreteXchange Research Program

**Status:** Proposed Research and Grant-Readiness Program
**Authority Boundary:** Research documents define hypotheses, preparation work, and possible studies. They do not claim scientific results, authorize production implementation, or expand an approved sprint.

## Purpose

The research library supports an ongoing program for technical validation, grant readiness, university and public-sector collaboration, commercialization diligence, and responsible environmental measurement.

## Program Objectives

- distinguish applied R&D from ordinary marketplace software development
- define falsifiable questions and measurable validation methods
- prepare reusable grant and partnership materials
- establish data, privacy, evidence, and uncertainty requirements early
- connect research results to customer value and commercialization gates
- prevent proposed models or metrics from being represented as implemented capabilities

## Opportunity Areas

- NSF opportunities
- EPA partnership opportunities
- DOE or DOT opportunities when solicitation topics align
- Texas regional or TCEQ-related opportunities
- municipal and regional partnerships
- university collaboration
- industry-sponsored research
- investor and enterprise diligence

No funding opportunity, eligibility, award, or deadline is asserted until verified against the current official solicitation.

## Research Governance

- Current production data and workflows remain governed by Project Context, CTX-ARCH documents, Product Decisions, privacy commitments, contracts, and applicable law.
- Research access requires a defined purpose, minimum necessary data, authorization, security, retention, publication, ownership, and licensing terms.
- Reported, corroborated, verified, estimated, and modeled information must remain distinguishable.
- Environmental and economic claims require defined boundaries, sources, methods, uncertainty, and limitations.
- A research prototype is not a production feature.
- A research hypothesis is not a result.
