## Review Cadence

- Review monthly while preparing an active opportunity.
- Reverify official solicitation dates, eligibility, and terms before submission.
- Record blockers, owners, evidence links, and next review dates in the working grant tracker.
- Do not change a status to Complete without reviewable evidence.
