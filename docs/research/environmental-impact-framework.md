# CreteXchange Environmental Impact Framework

**Status:** Proposed Measurement and Research Framework
**Boundary:** No environmental-impact engine, verified impact total, or Construction Circular Economy Index is currently implemented.

## Purpose

This framework defines how future environmental measurements should distinguish operational evidence from analytical estimates and communicate boundaries, uncertainty, and limitations.

## Measurement Principles

- Evidence Before Assumption
- transparent system boundaries
- source and factor provenance
- conservative interpretation
- uncertainty propagation
- reproducible calculations
- distinction among reported, corroborated, verified, estimated, and modeled data
- qualified language appropriate to coverage and confidence
- no double counting
- independent review for public or commercial claims

## Potential Input Data

- material type and classification confidence
- origin and destination
- facility acceptance and capability
- quantity and unit confidence
- geography and transportation distance
- processing or reuse outcome
- timing and capacity patterns
- supporting evidence and verification level
- applicable environmental factors and their source, date, geography, and uncertainty

## Proposed Measurement Classes

| Class | Meaning | Required Disclosure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reported | Supplied by a participant or source without independent confirmation | Source, date, scope, and absence of verification |
| Corroborated | Supported by more than one consistent source but not fully verified | Sources, corroboration method, unresolved gaps |
| Verified | Meets an approved evidence and validation standard | Standard, evidence, verifier, scope, and exceptions |
| Estimated | Calculated from observed inputs using documented assumptions or factors | Formula, factors, assumptions, uncertainty, and sensitivity |
| Modeled | Produced through a model or scenario beyond direct observation | Model, training/reference data, validation, assumptions, uncertainty, and limitations |

## Potential Measures

Future research may evaluate:

- material recovery rate
- landfill diversion
- verified reuse rate
- recycled-material utilization
- transportation efficiency
- participation growth
- data-confidence levels
- regional recovery infrastructure and access

Each measure requires a denominator, time period, geography, material scope, outcome definition, confidence treatment, and coverage disclosure. A label alone is not a method.

## Transportation and Scenario Analysis

Transportation analysis should distinguish observed routes or distances from estimated routing. Comparisons should account for baseline definition, vehicle assumptions, load, distance, regional conditions, and uncertainty. Virgin-versus-recycled or recovery-versus-disposal comparisons are scenarios unless supported by directly verified outcomes.

## Proposed Construction Circular Economy Index (`CCEI`)

`CCEI` is a proposed future analytical and benchmarking capability, subject to legal and branding review. Potential future products may include:

- regional benchmarking subscriptions
- contractor or facility scorecards
- industry reports
- sponsored studies
- custom benchmarking
- annual recognition programs

Any index must use transparent methodology; identify sources, coverage, funding, conflicts, revisions, and confidence; protect confidential data; prevent re-identification; and distinguish every measurement class above. Index publication requires separate Product Decisions, methodology approval, legal review, governance, and sufficient representative data.

## Validation Requirements

- domain-expert and independent methodology review
- documented source quality and factor versioning
- unit and boundary consistency checks
- uncertainty and sensitivity analysis
- comparison with credible external references where permitted
- prevention of duplicate events and double counting
- reproducibility and audit trail
- clear handling of missing data and selection bias
- participant and company confidentiality review

## Prohibited Claims

- presenting estimated or modeled outcomes as verified
- implying causation from correlation alone
- extrapolating beyond representative coverage without qualification
- publishing company-specific performance without authorization
- claiming environmental benefit without a defined baseline and method
- using `CCEI` as a current product or official brand before approval
