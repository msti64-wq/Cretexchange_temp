# CreteXchange Platform Economics

**Status:** Strategic Economics Framework
**Boundary:** Only the current $5 verified-drop foundation is an approved launch model. Other layers remain near-term, future, or exploratory until separately approved.

## Core Question

> Who receives value from the platform, what measurable value do they receive, and how can CreteXchange capture a reasonable portion of that value without discouraging participation?

## Funding Principle

Parties receiving measurable economic, operational, procurement, reporting, analytical, marketplace, or planning value should generally fund the platform. Participation friction should remain low for drivers and other users who strengthen network coverage, trust, transaction quality, and data value.

## Economic Validation Questions

- What measurable outcome changes for the customer?
- How is the value observed and over what period?
- Who uses the capability, who benefits, and who has purchasing authority?
- What alternative or manual cost does the customer face?
- What price or contract structure preserves participation and liquidity?
- What delivery, support, verification, data, and compliance costs apply?
- Does the revenue mechanism create bias, conflict, exclusion, or privacy risk?
- Can the service scale without fragmenting architecture or producing one-off work?

## Guardrails

- Preserve the current $5 launch foundation until an explicit Product Decision changes it.
- Keep driver participation free or low-friction whenever practical.
- Never alter canonical accounting through strategy documents.
- Do not bundle transaction, subscription, marketplace, referral, professional-service, and intelligence economics without explicit definitions.
- Do not create projections without sourced assumptions and sensitivity analysis.
- Do not monetize confidential company-specific data without authorization.
