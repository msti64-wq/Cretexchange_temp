# CreteXchange Research Roadmap

**Status:** Proposed Research Program Sequence
**Boundary:** This roadmap does not authorize production features or change Sprint 2.1.

## Phase R0 — Readiness and Governance

- recruit technical, environmental, geospatial, industry, and data-governance advisers
- complete literature, prior-art, and opportunity review
- define data rights, consent, privacy, security, retention, and publication controls
- inventory data quality, coverage, missingness, and bias
- select baselines, success criteria, and stop conditions

**Gate:** Research can proceed ethically, lawfully, securely, and with a meaningful validation design.

## Phase R1 — Evidence and Material Foundations

- evidence representation and provenance
- confidence semantics and simple baselines
- construction-material taxonomy and expert-labeling protocol
- duplicate definitions and operational review cases

**Gate:** Definitions are reproducible enough to support experiments.

## Phase R2 — Matching and Trust Research

- confidence-scoring candidates
- constraint-aware geospatial matching
- duplicate, anomaly, and suspected-fraud signals
- explainability and human-review workflows

**Gate:** Candidate methods demonstrate measurable value over baselines without unacceptable error or burden.

## Phase R3 — Environmental Measurement

- system boundaries and factor provenance
- uncertainty propagation
- transportation and recovery scenarios
- independent methodology review

**Gate:** Outputs clearly separate observed, reported, corroborated, verified, estimated, and modeled information.

## Phase R4 — Controlled Pilot

- bounded region, facilities, drivers, and approved material scope
- monitored research workflow
- performance, usability, privacy, safety, and failure evidence
- lessons learned and adverse-result reporting

**Gate:** Evidence supports a Phase II, revision, productization study, or stop decision.

## Phase R5 — Translation and Scale Research

- cross-region and cross-material generalization
- enterprise and public-sector decision validity
- marketplace behavior and liquidity studies
- representative benchmarking and possible index-method research

**Gate:** Coverage and methodology are sufficient for separately approved production or publication planning.

## Cross-Cutting Workstreams

- participant and partner research
- data governance and security
- statistical and AI/model validation
- geospatial validation
- environmental methodology
- commercialization and payer evidence
- IP, licensing, publication, and reproducibility
- grant, partnership, and investor diligence

## Decision Records

Each phase should conclude with evidence, limitations, unresolved risks, data changes, partner feedback, and a documented continue/revise/stop decision. Negative findings must not be suppressed to preserve a commercial narrative.
