# CreteXchange Customer Value Framework

**Status:** Canonical Customer-Value Reference

**Authority Boundary:** Guides product and business evaluation; it does not authorize a feature, price, data use, or expansion of current sprint scope.

## 1. Purpose

CreteXchange should create identifiable value for a defined customer before adding a major capability. The framework below separates current value from near-term, future, and exploratory opportunities.

## 3. Feature-Evaluation Framework

Before a significant feature is approved, its proposal should identify:

1. **Primary customer:** Who receives the principal value?
2. **Secondary beneficiaries:** Who else benefits or contributes network value?
3. **Value delivered:** What operational, commercial, procurement, reporting, analytical, marketplace, or planning outcome improves?
4. **Evidence of value:** What behavior, metric, research, or validation would demonstrate the improvement?
5. **Paying party:** Who should pay without discouraging valuable participation?
6. **Revenue model:** Is the proposed mechanism transactional, subscription, marketplace, service, licensing, referral, or non-revenue strategic value?
7. **Strategic objective:** Does it improve participation, verification, data quality, marketplace liquidity, defensibility, or another approved objective?
8. **Required data:** What minimum data and canonical sources are needed?
9. **Privacy implications:** What consent, access, confidentiality, aggregation, anonymization, retention, ownership, licensing, and disclosure controls apply?
10. **Current or future status:** Is the capability implemented, approved near-term work, future strategy, or exploratory?

Completion of this evaluation does not authorize implementation. Approval must still follow the applicable Product Decisions, CTX-ARCH documents, Platform Standards, Development Protocol, and sprint scope.
