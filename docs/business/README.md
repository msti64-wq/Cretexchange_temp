# CreteXchange Business Architecture

## Purpose

This library documents how CreteXchange creates customer value, sustains participation, evaluates monetization, develops network effects, and presents its long-term investment thesis.

## Scope

Business architecture guides customer-value and monetization decisions. It does not:

- override Platform Vision or Platform Strategy
- authorize product implementation or expand sprint scope
- override CTX-STD-001 or CTX-ARCH documents
- define accounting treatment, financial source-of-truth rules, APIs, or schemas
- claim that future revenue streams or services currently exist

## Intended Audience

- founders and product leadership
- architecture and engineering reviewers
- grant reviewers and investors
- strategic, industry, research, and technology partners
- municipalities, regional organizations, and agencies
- contributors evaluating significant product capabilities

## Status Labels

- **Current:** Implemented or part of the current approved launch foundation; exact availability remains governed by Project Context and production behavior.
- **Near-term:** Plausible next-stage direction requiring approved scope and validation.
- **Future:** Long-term strategic option requiring product and architecture approval.
- **Exploratory:** Hypothesis requiring substantial validation before roadmap commitment.

Future revenue opportunities in this library are strategic options, not current product claims, forecasts, or commitments.
