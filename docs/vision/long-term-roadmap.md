# CreteXchange Long-Term Roadmap

**Status:** Directional Strategic Roadmap
**Authority Boundary:** Translates [Platform Strategy](./platform-strategy.md) into stages. It does not authorize implementation, dates, budgets, or changes to [Sprint 2.1](../project/sprints/sprint-2.1.md).

## Roadmap Interpretation

Roadmap stages express increasing platform maturity. Work may overlap where validated, but no stage automatically enters a sprint. Project Context and approved sprint documents remain authoritative for current delivery.

## Stage 1 — Current Launch

**Status:** Current production and immediate approved focus, subject to Project Context and Sprint 2.1.

- driver experience
- participating washout and recycling-facility discovery
- documented eligible drop activity
- owner or facility verification
- driver rewards
- verification, duplicate prevention, confirmation, and dispute controls
- current $5 platform fee per verified load drop

**Gate:** Demonstrate trusted participation, repeat use, transaction quality, supportability, and coherent launch economics without disrupting driver workflows.

## Stage 2 — Material Recovery Foundation

**Status:** Approved direction and future capability; individual items require sprint approval.

- broader configurable material taxonomy
- material listings
- material buyers and sellers
- basic constraint-aware matching
- stronger transaction verification across approved material workflows

**Gate:** Validate supply, demand, material definitions, facility acceptance, transaction rules, and marketplace liquidity before broad commerce claims.

## Stage 3 — Intelligence Foundation

**Status:** Proposed research and future product direction.

- evidence-confidence scoring
- fraud and duplicate detection
- qualified environmental calculations
- operational analytics
- geospatial recovery recommendations

**Gate:** Demonstrate that methods are accurate, explainable, useful, privacy-governed, and superior to simpler baselines. Research prototypes are not production features.

## Stage 4 — Enterprise and Regional Intelligence

**Status:** Future strategy.

- contractor and enterprise dashboards
- government and regional dashboards
- reporting and operational integrations
- regional material-flow analytics
- enterprise and government APIs

**Gate:** Validate customer value and willingness to pay; approve security, access, procurement, privacy, methodology, integration, and architecture requirements.

## Stage 5 — National Intelligence

**Status:** Exploratory long-term strategy.

- multi-region analytics
- recovery-infrastructure gap analysis
- qualified national reporting
- Construction Circular Economy Index (`CCEI`), subject to future legal and branding review
- advanced forecasting and decision support

**Gate:** Establish representative coverage, transparent methodology, independent validation, governance, publication controls, and restrictions against unsupported environmental or performance claims.

## Capability Progression

```text
Verified Transactions
→ Verified Data
→ Operational Intelligence
→ Recovered-Material Marketplace
→ Enterprise SaaS
→ Government Intelligence
→ Construction Circular Economy Index
```

This progression is directional, not an automatic sprint sequence.

## Portfolio Guardrails

- Preserve the current launch path unless an explicit Product Decision changes it.
- Validate the transaction network before overbuilding intelligence or marketplace layers.
- Keep future capabilities labeled as future, proposed, or exploratory.
- Use research milestones to reduce uncertainty, not to imply scientific results.
- Require architecture updates before implementing capabilities outside current architecture.
- Keep Sprint 2.1 driver priorities unchanged.
