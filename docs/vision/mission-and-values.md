# CreteXchange Mission and Values

**Status:** Supporting Vision Reference
**Authority Boundary:** Interprets [Platform Vision](./platform-vision.md) and [Platform Strategy](./platform-strategy.md); it does not define current functionality or authorize roadmap work.

## Working Mission

> To accelerate the transition toward a circular construction economy by intelligently connecting people, businesses, materials, and environmental data within a trusted digital ecosystem.

## Working Vision

> To become the trusted intelligence platform that enables recoverable construction materials to be discovered, verified, reused, measured, and continuously improved throughout their lifecycle.

## Enduring Design Principles

### 1. Trust Before Transactions

Identity, authorization, evidence, verification, duplicate prevention, confirmation, disputes, and auditability should create confidence before the platform treats an event as trusted or billable.

### 2. Participation Before Monetization

Drivers and other participants who create network value should encounter little or no unnecessary participation friction. Revenue should generally come from parties receiving measurable economic, operational, procurement, reporting, analytical, or planning value.

### 3. Evidence Before Assumption

Operational, environmental, commercial, and policy claims should be traceable to defined sources and methods. Reported, corroborated, verified, estimated, and modeled information must remain distinguishable.

### 4. Circular Economy by Design

Discovery, recovery, movement, sale, reuse, and measurement should be designed as connected operational outcomes rather than added as unsupported sustainability language.

### 5. Scalable by Architecture

Platform growth should use configuration, canonical definitions, governed interfaces, and reusable domain boundaries rather than one-off workflows or duplicate logic.

### 6. Privacy and Stewardship

Data use should follow participant consent or another approved basis, purpose limitation, minimization, role-based access, confidentiality, aggregation, anonymization where appropriate, retention rules, auditability, and clear ownership and licensing terms.

### 7. Actionable Intelligence

Intelligence should improve a real decision or operational outcome. Analytical complexity without measurable participant value is not a strategic objective.

## Decision Use

These principles guide product, technical, research, environmental, partnership, grant, and commercial decisions. When principles compete, the proposal should document the tradeoff, affected participants, evidence, data requirements, and governing Product Decision.

## Current and Future Boundary

The current foundation remains driver-to-facility discovery, verified concrete washout activity, facility verification, driver rewards, and the current $5 platform fee per verified load drop under applicable architecture. Broader recovered-material commerce, advanced intelligence, public-sector products, research models, and the Construction Circular Economy Index are future or proposed capabilities.

## Branding Review Action

`Project Atlas` is a provisional internal transformation-program name under consideration. It is not adopted as an official program name. A future trademark and market-conflict review is required, alternative names may be considered, CreteXchange remains the product name, and Construction Circular Economy Intelligence Platform remains the long-term platform description.

The proposed acronym `CCEI` refers only to the future Construction Circular Economy Index and remains subject to legal and branding review.
