# Product Traceability

## Purpose

Product traceability ensures that significant features can be traced from strategic intent to delivery.

It protects the platform from drifting away from its vision and helps teams understand why a feature exists.

## Traceability Path

Every major feature should trace to:

Platform Vision

↓

Product Definition

↓

Product Decision(s)

↓

Operational Model

↓

Architecture

↓

Implementation

↓

Validation

↓

Release

## Why Traceability Matters

Traceability protects long-term quality by making decisions visible.

It helps the platform:

- preserve intent
- avoid accidental scope drift
- detect mismatches between product and implementation
- keep work reviewable over time
- support future expansion without breaking the foundation

## Maintenance Note

Every significant feature should have enough documentation to explain why it exists, how it works, and what operational value it creates.
