# CreteXchange Data Strategy

## 1. Data Strategy Philosophy

Every operational event should create value for multiple stakeholders.

Completed washouts, tips, check-ins, reward entries, and recycling activity should become insight, not just transaction history.

## 2. Relationship to Canonical Documents

- Platform Vision defines the long-term strategic direction for CreteXchange.
- Project Context summarizes the current implementation state and sprint roadmap.
- Product Decisions capture business direction and product intent.
- CTX-ARCH documents define the authoritative operational, financial, owner, driver, admin, and material mechanics that the data strategy must reflect.

## 3. Stakeholder Value

### Driver

- Ticket history
- Reward entries
- Location history
- Earnings and tip visibility
- Favorite or frequently used locations

### Owner

- Driver attraction metrics
- Repeat driver visits
- Average tip performance
- Reward entries generated by their site
- Site ranking and engagement indicators

### Admin / Platform

- Top-performing owner locations
- Highest-tipping locations
- Most-visited locations
- Reward-entry generation by site
- Driver engagement trends
- Market expansion opportunities
- Owner marketing opportunities

## 4. Event-to-Insight Map

| Event | Driver | Owner | Admin | Platform |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Completed washout | Confirms work completed, earnings context, and reward eligibility | Shows which site generated activity | Measures site output and driver activity | Feeds performance, usage, and growth analytics |
| Check-in | Confirms arrival and work traceability | Shows site traffic and driver visits | Tracks operational usage patterns | Supports location ranking and engagement analysis |
| Tip received | Shows earnings and compensation context | Shows tipping performance at the site | Tracks site-level generosity | Identifies high-value locations and driver economics |
| Reward entry earned | Shows ticket history and why an entry was issued | Shows reward generation by site | Tracks participation and reward generation | Builds engagement and incentive analytics |
| Drawing won | Shows prize outcome and recognition | Shows which sites contributed to wins indirectly | Supports drawing operations and reward reporting | Measures incentive effectiveness |
| Prize fulfilled | Shows reward delivery status | Shows successful reward outcome for the site ecosystem | Tracks fulfillment efficiency | Supports customer experience and operational performance |
| Rubble drop-off, future | Could show another type of earned activity | Could show recycling-driven engagement | Could measure recycling participation | Could support sustainability and routing analytics |

## 5. Future Admin Marketing / Insights Page

CreteXchange should eventually include an admin-facing marketing and insights page showing:

- Top driver-attracting sites
- Highest-tipping sites
- Most reward entries generated
- Repeat driver rate
- Total visits by owner/location
- Owner engagement ranking
- Regional performance
- Monthly trends

## 6. Export Requirements

The future admin insights page should support:

- CSV export
- Downloadable reports
- Filtered date ranges
- Owner and location filters
- Regional filters
- Monthly summaries

## 7. Future Owner Outreach Automation

Future capability should include:

- Automated owner marketing emails
- Monthly owner performance reports
- "Your site attracted X drivers" summaries
- "Your average tip ranks in the top X%" summaries
- Recommendations to improve driver attraction
- Opt-in or controlled rollout

## 8. Guardrails

- Do not expose private driver personal information unnecessarily.
- Aggregate data where possible.
- Avoid public rankings that shame owners.
- Use rankings as constructive business intelligence.
- Respect privacy, security, and role permissions.

## 9. Relationship to Driver Rewards

Reward tickets should show the activity and location where they were earned.

This improves driver transparency and creates useful metadata for platform analytics.

Ticket history becomes part of the data foundation for future owner and admin insights.
