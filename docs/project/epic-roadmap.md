# CreteXchange Epic Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap defines the major bodies of work that organize related sprints and long-term platform capabilities.

Epics organize related outcomes; sprints authorize implementation. Future epics are directional and do not imply current functionality.

## Epic 1 — Driver Experience

**Status:** Complete for Sprint 2.1 scope

- Driver rewards and dashboard intelligence
- Location discovery
- Check-in and activity experience
- Notifications
- Profile and payout readiness
- Driver security and privacy hardening

Remaining financial and UX remediation must be separately scoped. This status does not declare every driver workflow or financial lifecycle complete.

## Epic 2 — Owner Intelligence

**Status:** Complete for Sprint 2.1.3 scope

- Owner dashboard intelligence
- Location intelligence
- Driver intelligence
- Operational reporting
- Owner privacy boundaries

See [Sprint 2.1](./sprints/sprint-2.1.md) for the completed scope.

## Epic 3 — Platform Operations

**Status:** Complete for Sprint 2.1.4 scope

- Platform Growth
- Trust & Verification
- Platform Activity
- Marketplace Health & Readiness
- Operational exceptions and platform administration

See [Sprint 2.1.4](./sprints/sprint-2.1.4.md). This delivered epic is operational intelligence, not financial implementation.

## Epic 4 — MVP Operational Readiness

**Status:** Active / Next

- First driver and facility onboarding readiness
- Facility configuration and discoverability readiness
- Verified-transaction completion readiness
- Pilot operations, support, and release confidence

See [Sprint 2.2](./sprints/sprint-2.2.md). This epic prioritizes successful first production users over major new platform capabilities.

## Epic 5 — Financial Platform

**Status:** Planned and architecture-governed

- Canonical driver incentive snapshot
- Payment obligation and owner charge
- Wallet-authoritative settlement
- Stripe payout rail
- Idempotency, reconciliation, billing, reporting, and KPI alignment

[PD-045](../product/product-decisions.md#pd-045---canonical-driver-settlement-rail) and [CTX-ARCH-006](../architecture/driver-incentive-and-financial-settlement-architecture.md) govern this epic. Current mixed settlement behavior requires remediation; documentation does not mean that implementation is complete.

## Epic 6 — Recovered-Material Marketplace

**Status:** Future

- Recovered-material listings
- Sellers, buyers, inventory, and availability
- Matching and discovery
- Reservation or transaction workflows
- Logistics coordination and marketplace trust

## Epic 7 — Enterprise and Contractor Platform

**Status:** Future

- Project material tracking and procurement
- Diversion and reuse reporting
- Enterprise dashboards
- Integrations and APIs
- Supplier and facility network management

## Epic 8 — Regional and Government Intelligence

**Status:** Future

- Aggregated regional dashboards and material-flow analytics
- Infrastructure-gap analysis and policy-support reporting
- Municipality and agency services
- Privacy and anonymization controls

## Epic 9 — Construction Circular Economy Intelligence

**Status:** Long-term strategic destination

- Intelligence domains and benchmarking
- Construction Circular Economy Index
- Research partnerships
- Environmental and economic scenario analysis
- Transparent evidence-confidence methodology

## Cross-Epic Principles

- Verified transaction network first
- Low participation friction
- One canonical source of truth per domain
- Privacy and data minimization
- Auditable financial architecture
- Evidence-based environmental claims
- Current-versus-future capability separation
- Product Decision and architecture governance

## Authority Boundary

This roadmap organizes outcomes and dependencies. It does not authorize implementation or override [Platform Vision](../vision/platform-vision.md), [Platform Strategy](../vision/platform-strategy.md), [Project Context](./project-context.md), Platform Standards, CTX-ARCH documents, or Product Decisions.
